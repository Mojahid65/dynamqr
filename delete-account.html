Delete Your DynamQR Account

Request deletion of your DynamQR account and associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. 1 Open the DynamQR mobile app on your smartphone or device.
  2. 2 Sign in to your registered DynamQR account.
  3. 3 Navigate to the Settings / Profile menu.
  4. 4 Scroll down and select “Delete Account”.
  5. 5 Confirm the account deletion request when prompted to permanently erase your data.

What Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, the following personal data and records associated with your profile will be permanently purged:

  • DynamQR account information (account record and login credentials)
  • Email address associated with the DynamQR account
  • User profile information (name, avatar, and preferences)
  • QR codes created and stored in your account history
  • QR code configuration & routing data associated with your account
  • Account-related data stored in our primary production database

What May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained temporarily when necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or fulfilling mandatory regulatory obligations.

Any retained information will strictly be kept only for as long as necessary to satisfy the specific legal or security purpose, after which it will be permanently deleted from our archives.

If You Cannot Access Your Account

If you have lost access to your device, forgotten your credentials, or are unable to log into the DynamQR app, you can request manual account deletion by contacting our support team directly.

Contact Support via Email: support@mojahidhassan.in Please send your email from the address associated with your DynamQR account so we can verify account ownership.

Important Information

  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, you will lose all access to created dynamic QR codes.
  • Deleting an account removes the associated account data according to DynamQR's data retention practices.
  • Users should export or save any dynamic QR configurations or analytics they need before deleting their account.
